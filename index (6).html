Burger Franchise Comparison

Lotteria, Burger King, and McDonald’s compared by franchise-only store count. This version uses recent disclosure reporting and makes the distinction between a disclosure/publication year and the underlying data year explicit.

The primary comparison is store count, not company revenue. Company-owned stores are excluded. The figures below are based on recent disclosure reporting located during research; the original FTC brand records should be checked before treating the table as a final official dataset.

Important year distinction: “2025 disclosure” is not automatically the same as “stores counted during calendar year 2025.” The FTC’s 2024 industry statistics release, published on April 9, 2025, explicitly says its franchisee counts and average sales are based on information for 2023. Always record both the disclosure/publication year and the data base year.

4. What the comparison suggests

Using the reported franchise-only counts shown above, Lotteria has the largest franchise network of these three brands, followed by Burger King and McDonald’s. The difference is partly about operating structure: a brand that owns most of its restaurants can have a large total network but relatively few franchise stores.

These counts measure franchise network size only. They do not show profitability, company revenue, sales per store, or overall brand success. A sales comparison needs average franchisee revenue figures from the same base year and the same definition.

6. Original and official sources

  1. Korea Fair Trade Commission — franchise information comparison. Official franchise disclosure search interface; use it to verify brand-level franchise and company-owned counts and average sales.
  2. Korea Fair Trade Commission — 2024 franchise business statistics release. Published April 9, 2025; explains that the store-count and average-sales data in that release are based on 2023 disclosure information.
  3. Korea Public Data Portal — FTC brand-level franchise/store and average-sales data. Official dataset description with brand, franchise-store count, company-owned store count, average sales, and accounting-year fields.
  4. Burger King Korea disclosure profile. Secondary summary reporting 472 total stores, including 125 franchise and 347 company-owned stores, for the 2024 disclosure reference.
  5. Seoul Window — Korean fast-food franchise history. Secondary article reporting 1,209 Lotteria franchise stores and 55 McDonald’s franchise stores in its discussion of 2025 disclosure figures; use FTC records for final verification.
